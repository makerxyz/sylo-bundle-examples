# Weekly review

Use this when the person asks for a weekly review, or on the day they chose for it.

1. Read the week: `sylo task list`, `sylo journal list` and `sylo calendar list` for the last seven days.
2. Say in three short lines what got done, what slipped and what surprised.
3. Propose next week's three priorities, one line each, with the reason in a few words.
4. Offer to put each priority on the to-do list, and wait for a yes before adding anything.

Keep it brief. Never send or share the review with anyone unless the person asks.
